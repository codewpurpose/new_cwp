# CWP homepage design

Keep the existing CodeWithPurpose homepage content, section order, destinations, and navigation. The playful treatment is a warm sand background, softly rounded Fredoka headings, and Koda in the hero. Body copy keeps the site's existing font.

Preserve the impact figures, course links, stories, media, FAQs, calls to action, footer links, account actions, and their existing routes. Simplify the visual weight with open layouts, quiet separators, and fewer card surfaces. Keep the existing responsive behavior and form interactions. Koda can cycle through poses on click and uses a brief, gentle entrance motion that respects reduced-motion preferences.

This style applies only to the homepage. All other pages retain their existing layouts, functionality, fonts, and design tokens.
